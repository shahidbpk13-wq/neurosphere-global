NeuroSphere Horizon

The long-horizon build — designed to raise against.

A licensed pediatric neurodevelopment clinic, a precision-genetics service, an EEG/neuroimaging platform, and an AI-guided rehabilitation product. This is a concept package for hospital partners and investors — not a plan to execute this quarter.

Discuss a partnership

Framing. This is the founder's long-horizon thesis — the reason a genetics service, an EEG company, and a rehab platform belong under one roof — kept ready so that when the right hospital partner or funding source appears, the group doesn't have to be designed from scratch.

Division A

Clinical Neurodevelopment & Genetics

NDCC — NeuroDevelopment & Communication Center

A hybrid clinic, telehealth, and school-integrated center for pediatric speech/language therapy and neurodevelopment, anchored by a signature Epilepsy-Associated Developmental Pathway.

Genetics & AI-Powered Precision Medicine

Clinical genetics, personalized counseling, and AI-based genomic analysis — WGS/WES, CNV analysis, pharmacogenomics, and variant pathogenicity prediction.

Division B

NeuroTechnology Platforms

Brain2Brain Solutions

Continuous ICU EEG monitoring with AI-assisted anomaly detection, plus DTI/fMRI/structural MRI neuroimaging processing and consultation services.

RehabitAI

AI-driven personalized rehabilitation plans, wearable-based recovery tracking, and predictive recovery-timeline forecasting — the discharge pathway from Brain2Brain and NDCC.

Honest assessment

Regulatory pathway, stated plainly

Regulatory timelines, not technology or clinical demand, are the primary constraint on this division. NDCC and Genetics require Saudi facility/lab licensing before opening. Brain2Brain and RehabitAI, where they inform diagnosis or treatment, fall under Software-as-a-Medical-Device regulation — typically a 1–3 year clearance process. The recommended path is a single, narrowly-scoped SaMD use case first, while Corporate Wellness Screening and the Second-Opinion Network generate non-regulated revenue on the same underlying assets.
